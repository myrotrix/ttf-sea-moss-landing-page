What is GLP-III™?

GLP-III™ is a capsule-form dietary supplement concept from The Transformation Factory, bringing together Gymnema Sylvestre, Berberine Complex, and OEA in its working formula.

It's not a prescription GLP-1 medication. The name doesn't mean it acts like one, and no drug-equivalent effects are being claimed.

What is GLP-III™?

GLP-III™ is the working name of a new TTF dietary supplement in capsule form. The final labeling and launch materials are still under review.

Is GLP-III™ a GLP-1 prescription drug?

No. This product is a dietary supplement concept, not a prescription GLP-1 medication. The names do not imply the same effects.

Which ingredients are included?

The draft label identifies Gymnema Sylvestre, a Berberine Complex and Oleoylethanolamide (OEA). Formula specifications and label text must be verified before launch.

How do I take it?

Final directions are not being provided here because the working documents have conflicting dosage instructions. Please rely only on future approved product labeling.

When will it be available and how much will it cost?

A launch date, price and any subscription terms have not been approved for publication. This page is an early product-story preview only.

Yes. For general questions, contact support@seamosstransformation.com.
